Your subscription will automatically renew within 24 hours prior to the end of the current billing cycle. Carva reserves the right to adjust subscription pricing. You can cancel auto-renewal at any time. To avoid being charged for the next cycle, you must cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Uninstalling the app or deleting your account will not cancel your subscription.

How to Cancel: Open the Google Play Store app > Tap Profile > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions > Select Carva > Cancel.

When you cancel a subscription, you will continue to have access to Carva Pro features until the end of your current billing cycle. We do not provide pro-rated refunds for the remaining days of a canceled cycle, unless strictly required by applicable law or Google Play policy.
