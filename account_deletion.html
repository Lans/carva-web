Carva 账号与数据注销

At Carva, we respect your privacy and data ownership. If you wish to permanently delete your account and all associated expense data, you can do so through the methods below in compliance with Google Play's Data Safety guidelines.

⚠️ Important Notice Regarding Subscriptions:
Deleting your account will NOT automatically cancel your active Carva Pro subscription. You must manually cancel your auto-renewal in the Google Play Store to avoid future charges.

Method 1: Delete via the Carva App (Fastest)

If you still have the app installed, you can delete your account immediately:

  1. Open the Carva app on your device.
  2. Navigate to Settings (Profile).
  3. Tap on Account Management.
  4. Select Delete Account and confirm your choice.

Your Firebase Auth account and all cloud-synced expense data will be wiped instantly and cannot be recovered.

Method 2: Request Deletion via Web/Email

If you have already uninstalled the app and wish to delete your account, please send an email to our support team from your registered email address. We will process your request within 7-14 business days.

What Data Gets Deleted?

Upon successful deletion, the following data will be permanently removed from our servers (Google Firebase / Cloud):

  • Your account credentials and authentication data.
  • All your EV expense records, charging logs, and generated reports.
  • Your custom avatars and preferences.

Note: We may retain certain transaction records (e.g., billing history for Carva Pro) strictly for legal, tax, or accounting purposes as required by law.

哪些数据会被删除？

注销完成后，以下数据将从我们的服务器 (Google Firebase) 中被永久彻底抹除：

  • 您的账号登录凭证与身份验证信息。
  • 您的所有电车记账记录、充电日志以及生成的年度报告。
  • 您自定义的头像与应用偏好设置。

注：根据相关法律法规要求，我们可能会将某些脱敏的交易记录（如 Carva Pro 的支付流水）保留一段特定的时间，仅用于税务或财务审计目的。
