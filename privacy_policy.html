Carva 隐私政策

Last Updated: September 2026

Carva values your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your data.

1. Information We Collect

To provide better services, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Notification Permission: Used to send you important billing reminders and updates. You can turn off notifications in system settings at any time.
  • Camera & Storage Permission: Allows you to take photos of receipts and access local files to save or load app data (e.g., avatars and generated report posters). Photos taken are processed securely.
  • Account & Device Data: We collect authentication data via Firebase, along with anonymous diagnostic data to improve app stability.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is only used to provide and optimize app features, including but not limited to:

  • Providing core app features such as EV expense tracking and AI reporting.
  • Enhancing user experience and optimizing app performance.
  • Supporting ad delivery to provide you with relevant advertising content.
  • Conducting app troubleshooting and issue resolution.

3. Data Storage and Protection

We take the following measures to protect your data security:

  • All data is stored and transmitted using industry-standard encryption technology.
  • Strict access controls are implemented to protect your expense data.
  • Security measures are regularly updated to address new security threats.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to optimize app functionality:

  • Google Analytics & Firebase: Used to collect anonymous usage data to analyze and optimize the app experience.
  • Google Ads: Used to display personalized advertisements. You can turn off personalized ads in your device settings.
  • Google ML Kit & Vertex AI: Used for receipt OCR and intelligent analysis. Data is processed solely for your features and is not used to train public AI models.

5. Your Rights

According to relevant laws and regulations, you have the following rights:

  • Access, view, correct, or update your personal information.
  • Request the permanent deletion of your account and personal data (available within the App Settings).
  • Withdraw consent for specific data processing activities.

6. Disclaimer

While we implement robust security measures, no electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. We cannot be held liable for data loss, leakage, or unauthorized access resulting from force majeure, device vulnerabilities (e.g., rooting/jailbreaking), or third-party service outages beyond our reasonable control. Additionally, our OCR and AI features process data for your convenience, but we do not guarantee the absolute accuracy of the generated results. Please verify critical financial figures manually.

7. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and discover that your child has provided us with personal information without your consent, please contact us immediately, and we will take steps to delete such information.

8. Policy Updates

We may update this privacy policy from time to time to comply with the latest laws and regulations or business needs. Updated privacy policies will be published on this page. Your continued use of our services constitutes your acceptance of the updated privacy policy.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions, suggestions, or complaints about this privacy policy, please contact us via email at: support@carva-app.com.
